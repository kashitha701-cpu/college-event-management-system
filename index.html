About College Event Management System

The College Event Management System is a web platform created to bring all college events together in one place. Students can easily discover upcoming events, read event details, and register online without any confusion.

For college organizers and faculty, the system makes it simpler to publish events, manage participants, and keep a permanent record of previously conducted events. This saves time, reduces paperwork, and improves participation across the campus.
